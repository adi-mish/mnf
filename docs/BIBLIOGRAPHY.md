# Working bibliography

This scaffold was designed around the following research directions:

- Causal abstraction as a formal basis for mechanistic interpretability.
- The non-linear representation dilemma: causal abstraction can become vacuous without naturalness constraints.
- Transformer circuits, induction heads, IOI, ACDC, and activation/path patching.
- Sparse autoencoders, SAE scaling, Gemma Scope, SAEBench, feature splitting/absorption, and random-transformer SAE controls.
- Typed / multidimensional feature geometry, especially cyclic features.
- Transcoders, cross-layer transcoders, attribution graphs, and sparse mixtures of linear transforms.
- Causal tracing, ROME, and factual-association editing.
- Refusal directions and high-level control variables.
- Functional emotion concepts in Claude Sonnet 4.5.
